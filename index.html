ABOUT THE WORKSHOP

Build models.
Deepen your understanding.

The CNU–Certara RsNLME Workshop offers two days of practical learning in population modeling. The program moves from setting up NLME models in R to model evaluation, simulation and extended PK/PD workflows.

Population PK/PDModel evaluationSimulationHands-on exercises

SCIENTIFIC PROGRAM

From foundations to application.

DAY 01 / MONDAY
22
FEBRUARY 2027

Introduction
to RsNLME

Overview of RsNLME and hands-on exercises to establish the foundations of the R workflow.

  1. 01Overview of RsNLME and hands-on exercises
  2. 02Setting up and running NLME models using R
  3. 03Standard population PK models
  4. 04Below Limit of Quantification (BLQ)
  5. 05Model summaries
  6. 06Model evaluation plots
  7. 07Visual Predictive Checks (VPCs)
DAY 02 / TUESDAY
23
FEBRUARY 2027

Extending the
RsNLME Workflow

More in-depth coverage of the R workflow for RsNLME with additional hands-on exercises.

  1. 01In-depth R workflow for RsNLME
  2. 02Standard population PK/PD models
  3. 03Multiple-response models
  4. 04Simulation
  5. 05Bootstrap
  6. 06Stepwise Covariate Modeling (SCM)
