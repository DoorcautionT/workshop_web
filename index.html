ABOUT THE WORKSHOP

Build models.
Deepen your understanding.


CNU–Certara RsNLME Workshop is a hands-on training program.
The program progresses from setting up and running nonlinear mixed-effects (NLME) models in R to model evaluation, simulation and advanced PK/PD modeling.

01 / 04

Foundations

Gain practical experience with R and RsNLME, from model building to interpreting results.

02 / 04

Model evaluation

Assess models using diagnostic plots and visual predictive checks.

03 / 04

Simulation

Predict drug concentrations and responses under different conditions.

04 / 04

Population PK/PD modeling

Analyze drug concentrations and responses and explore variability between individuals.

  1. 01RsNLME overview and hands-on exercises

  3. 03Standard population pharmacokinetic (PK) models
  4. 04Handling below-the-limit-of-quantification (BLQ) data

  6. 06Model evaluation plots
